The year, at a glance

The cohort relationship runs on a steady cadence. Ongoing work continues all year; the two formal observation rounds anchor each term and feed the Teachers' Expectations evaluations.

ONGOING

Plans & presence

Review lessons and units; drop in for informal observations; keep the coaching relationship warm.

TERM 1

Formal · Round 1

One scheduled formal observation per teacher, scored on the separate observation form, with a growth goal set together.

MID-YEAR

1-1 & input

Mentoring and feedback meetings; provide insights toward each teacher's Expectations evaluation.

TERM 2

Formal · Round 2

Second formal observation; measure movement on the goal and close the year's evidence.

A note on the model

Instructional coaches and student-success leads (Paulina Ortiz, Erika Pérez) lead a cohort but are not themselves observed within one. Rocío Talayero carries a small cohort on each lower campus. Roles still being filled are held as TBD placeholders.

Scope. Cohorts cover homeroom and specialist teachers only. Assistants, SPED teachers, counselors, deans, librarians, full-time subs and lifeguards are not assigned to cohorts.

Source. 2026-27 staff plantilla. Middle and High School are tracked as one combined pool. Names marked TBD are open positions held by their teaching slot.
